The Challenge & Strategy

Dissecting Business Pain Points & User Needs

Any successful product begins with a deep understanding of the business environment and target audience. This section deconstructs the core challenges ATEN faced and outlines the user-centered design strategy we developed in response.

At the Crossroads of Business Transformation

At the project's inception, the company was at a critical transformation juncture, facing two major business challenges:

1. Market Disconnection

The heavy reliance on distributors for single-product sales made it difficult for the company to grasp end-customer needs and behavioral data. This gap not only compressed product margins but also limited the cultivation of customer loyalty.

2. High Proposal Barrier

Pro AV system architectures are extremely complex. New sales personnel or System Integrators (SI) required a lengthy learning curve to independently complete a professional proposal, directly leading to extended sales cycles and low efficiency.

UX as a Strategic Driver

Facing these challenges, the role of UX extended beyond interface optimization to become a strategic core driving revenue growth. Our key proposition was:

"How can we lower the proposal barrier for SIs and improve proposal success rates?"

Solution Builder was our answer — a digital tool designed to standardize, visualize, and automate the complex system planning process. It not only solved the efficiency problem but became the company's strategic pivot for directly reaching end-users and driving solution-based sales.

Target Audience & Layered Design Strategy

To ensure the product precisely met different users' needs, we defined three core target personas and tailored a layered design strategy for each:

Novice SI

Pain Point

Unfamiliar with product lines, unsure how to combine products, lacks confidence in proposals.

Strategy: Guided Templates

We designed a wizard-like guided experience that abstracts product complexity. Users only need to answer simple scenario questions (e.g., "Medium Conference Room"), and the system generates an effective solution draft — enabling newcomers to create professional proposals in minutes instead of days.

Expert SI

Pain Point

Pursues maximum efficiency, needs to quickly handle complex architectures and verify compatibility.

Strategy: Flexible Canvas + Real-time Validation

We provided an unrestricted drag-and-drop canvas for maximum expert efficiency. Crucially, we embedded a compatibility engine that provides real-time feedback on connections, allowing rapid prototyping and risk mitigation for complex custom architectures.

Internal Sales / Subsidiaries

Pain Point

Need to quickly respond to small-to-medium cases and reduce internal training costs.

Strategy: Automated Proposal Generation

The system automates the last mile — generating complete documents including BOM (Bill of Materials) and system architecture diagrams. This frees sales teams from tedious paperwork, allowing them to focus on high-value client communication.

Product Features & Design Details

Core Features Deep Dive

These features were meticulously designed to fundamentally disrupt the traditional, time-consuming analog workflow, reshaping it into an intuitive, intelligent digital experience.

Feature 1. Visual Topology Editor

The Heart of the Application

A powerful visual canvas that allows users to build system architectures as easily as drawing on a whiteboard.

Technical Foundation

Built with React framework and deeply customized on top of @xyflow/react library, ensuring smooth interaction and high extensibility.

Intuitive Operation

Users can drag and drop device icons from the right-side device library onto the canvas for rapid layout composition.

Rich Editing Tools

Device Icons: Four different sizes (Full Detail, L, S, T (Text-only)), allowing users to freely switch detail levels based on diagram complexity and presentation needs.

Device Labels: A unique colored-border system that lets users tag devices as Core (orange), Main (blue), Auxiliary (green), or Unresolved Issue (red), making complex architecture diagrams instantly comprehensible.

Auxiliary Graphics: Built-in annotation tools and background shapes including text, circles, and rectangular containers, helping users create professional, highly readable system diagrams.

Smart Connections

Users simply drag between device ports to establish connections. The system automatically colors lines based on preset signal-type color schemes (Video: blue, Audio: pink, Network: light blue), dramatically improving diagram readability and professionalism.

Feature 2. Flexible Project Creation

Dual-Path Design for All Skill Levels

Start from a Template

This mode provides a wizard-like guided experience. Users select a preset application scenario from the homepage, such as "Conference Room" or "Classroom." Through the RoomConditionSelector component, users further select specific conditions and additional equipment — the system then auto-generates a pre-configured diagram that only needs minor adjustments.

Build from Scratch

For senior experts, this mode provides a completely blank canvas. Users can design highly customized solutions without any restrictions, fully leveraging their professional expertise.

This dual-path design approach was a direct outcome of our user research, ensuring the tool is immediately accessible to novice SIs through templates while providing experts with unlimited creative freedom.

Feature 3. Automated Documentation

Automating the Last Mile of the Proposal Process

BOM Generation

The "Export BOM" feature exports all products and quantities from the canvas into a standard Excel Bill of Materials with a single click — eliminating tedious manual counting and errors.

System Architecture Diagram Export

The "Export Diagram" feature generates a professional PDF document from the current canvas content, allowing users to customize author, date, and other information for direct use in formal proposals.

Smart BOM Calculation

The system features advanced calculation logic. BOM calculations precisely account for device quantities within "group" nodes (including multipliers) and automatically filter nodes marked as "hidden" — ensuring final document accuracy.

Technical Implementation & Contribution

Crossing the Boundary Between Design and Development

As a UX Engineer, my contribution extended beyond drafting design blueprints to hands-on implementation. This section details the key engineering tasks I undertook — work that not only realized the design vision but solved a foundational data problem blocking project progress, while building a robust, scalable frontend architecture.

Engineering 1. Frontend Architecture & Component Development

Building a Modular, Scalable System

Technology Stack

React as core framework, Ant Design as UI component library, and @xyflow/react for the visual diagram editing functionality.

Custom Node Components

I created highly customized React components for canvas elements, such as DeviceNode and GroupNode. This approach transformed canvas nodes from static icons into intelligent, self-contained agents. Each node manages its own data fetching, view management, and port rendering logic — creating a highly modular and extensible architecture.

Performance Optimization

A critical UX challenge was the product library's scale — thousands of devices. Traditional loading would cause unacceptable initial load times. I implemented a batch-request lazy-loading strategy through the getDeviceList function, ensuring the app becomes interactive within seconds while the complete device library loads seamlessly in the background.

Engineering 2. AI-Driven Product Data Unification

Solving the Legacy Data Bottleneck with LLMs

The Problem

The project was initially blocked by a critical data problem: decades of legacy product specifications existed only in unstructured, inconsistent HTML tables, making them unusable by modern applications. This became a massive bottleneck.

The Solution

I developed a Node.js script to automate data cleansing and structuring. The script fetches messy product specification tables from internal APIs and leverages the OpenAI API (GPT-4o) to transform them into clean, unified JSON format.

Precision Prompt Engineering

The solution's success hinged on meticulous prompt engineering. I designed detailed prompts with strict rules to guide the AI in handling complex scenarios such as: parsing quantity expressions ("2 x HDMI"), accurately classifying port types, and correctly identifying special devices like signal extenders — categorizing their unique "Unit to Unit" interfaces as video inputs or outputs.

This not only solved the data problem but demonstrated the technical depth of leveraging Large Language Models to solve real-world business challenges.

Engineering 3. Elegant Solutions for Complex Features

Implementation Highlights

Atomic BOM Download

To solve the "download BOM directly from template preview" requirement, I designed an elegant flow: when users click download, the system creates a temporary project in the backend, passes the BOM calculated from visible nodes, triggers Excel export, then immediately deletes the temporary project.

This design provides the instant gratification of direct downloads while avoiding orphaned or temporary projects in the user's workspace — maintaining a clean, purpose-driven UX.

Batch Automation Processing

The handleBatchProcess function is a powerful internal tool that automatically generates all permutations of a template's options and executes a series of automated actions (e.g., standardizing line thickness, saving projects) on each combination.

This showcases architectural foresight, providing the internal team with tremendous convenience for large-scale template management and maintenance.

Validation & Business Impact

Data-Driven Value Measurement

A project's success depends not only on usability but on its substantive contribution to business goals. We established a quantifiable direct link connecting user engagement to core business outcomes such as lead generation and sales conversion — something previously untrackable.

Defining Success Metrics

Efficiency Improvement

Based on internal team testing, proposal preparation time was dramatically reduced from an average of 3 days to 30 minutes.

Marketing & Sales Funnel

Acquisition: The tool itself became a powerful lead magnet, attracting a significant number of new System Integrators to register, effectively expanding the prospect list.

Retention: We tracked high-value SI users' platform engagement and return rates to measure product stickiness.

Conversion: The system converts draft diagrams created in the simulator directly into Salesforce opportunities, seamlessly integrating with the sales process.

Establishing the Data Feedback Loop

Data Architecture

We used GA4 on the frontend for precise user behavior tracking, streamed data to BigQuery for storage and analysis, and ultimately integrated with CRM sales data in PowerBI to form a complete business insight dashboard.

Insights to Action

Through this data system, we gained invaluable insights that translated into concrete business actions:

Market Demand Insights

After analyzing massive diagram data, we discovered the most popular product combinations in the market. This directly prompted the marketing department to launch targeted "Product Bundle" sales campaigns.

Product R&D Guidance

When we discovered users frequently resorting to third-party solutions to fill functionality gaps for specific connection needs, we fed this data back to the R&D team. This directly led to the development of several new products, successfully converting market demand into new revenue drivers for the company.
