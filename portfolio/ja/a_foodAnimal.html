Food Animal Personality Quiz

Side Project

"If what we eat defines who we are, what animal would we be?"

This project originated from a casual lunchtime conversation among colleagues, yet became the perfect testing ground for validating "AI-collaborative full-stack development." Unlike traditional commercial projects burdened with heavy KPIs, the original intent was pure and challenging: How can one person, without engineers or illustrators, leverage Generative AI to create a commercial-grade interactive experience?

This is not just a personality quiz — it's a cross-disciplinary productivity experiment. As the initiator, I integrated product strategy, Prompt Engineering, and frontend development to address common pain points in online quizzes: inconsistent visual styles and loosely structured copy.

Core Value & Engagement Strategy

AI Orchestration: Demonstrating how to chain Midjourney and LLMs together, compressing a development cycle from weeks to days.

Design System Consistency: Overcoming the inherent randomness of AI generation to establish a highly consistent visual and copywriting system.

Key Engagement Strategies

1. Animal Metaphor

Abstract personality traits were transformed into concrete "animal personas" (e.g., Strategist Elephant, Slow-Warm Cat). Compared to cold adjectives, animal imagery is far more memorable and entertaining, quickly establishing emotional connections with users.

2. "The One-liner" — Instant Gut Feel

Each result page features a signature "inner monologue" at the top (e.g., Logic Swan: "Regarding the order of desserts, I have an Excel spreadsheet that can explain it."). This lets users intuitively grasp the personality essence within 3 seconds, dramatically increasing the desire to screenshot and share.

3. Social Compatibility Mechanic

I specifically designed "Best Dining Buddy" and "Dining Disaster" animal pairings. This creates a powerful social interaction motive — transforming a one-way quiz into a two-way social tagging and sharing experience.

Research & Logic Framework

Translating "Taste" into "Personality"

To prevent the quiz from devolving into random guessing, I introduced an MBTI-inspired framework, translating vague dietary preferences into a quantifiable "four-dimension model."

The Four Dimensions of Dining Personality

Taste vs. Idea

Do you eat for physical deliciousness or story and atmosphere?

Social vs. Lonely

Is dining about connecting with others or recharging yourself?

Deep-rooted vs. Experimental

Faced with "Cilantro Century Egg Pizza" — do you defend Italian dignity, or get excited to try it?

Methodical vs. Free-flowing

When ordering, do you calculate nutritional ratios, or go with the moment's mood?

Question Design Example

Scenario: A dessert shop just launched new items — how do you choose?

A. Classic strawberry cake (tried it many times, definitely delicious!)
→ Deep-rooted (Conservative & Committed)

B. Matcha chili cheese puff (I just have to know what this tastes like!)
→ Experimental (Adventurous & Exploring)

Content Engineering

Giving Data a Soul with Prompt Chains

I leveraged LLMs playing the role of a "humorous food critic," using Prompt Chaining techniques to transform cold logic labels into vivid character profiles. I strictly followed a Human-in-the-loop model: AI handled creative divergence; I handled convergence and refinement.

Visual Consistency System

Taming Midjourney's Randomness

The biggest challenge with AI-generated images is "style drift." To make all 16 result cards look like they belong to the same illustrated guide, I conducted extensive Prompt stress tests and established a visual specification anchored on the "Sumikko Gurashi style."

Prompt Engineering Key Parameters

Style Anchor: pastel sketchy style, minimalist graphics, bold outlines.

Atmosphere: Clean solid warm cream background, soft lighting.

Character-Specific Fine-tuning

Comfort Cat (Slow-Warm Cat)

Blended pastel soft koala roundness traits, adding "curled up in a kotatsu blanket" to reinforce the "homebody" attribute.

Hedgehog Soloist

Added "clean lab-like setting" and "reading nutrition label" — making visual details echo the character profile.

Technical Execution

Pair Programming with AI

During the development phase, I played the role of "architect," guiding AI (Copilot/LLM) to write core logic.

Data Structure Design (JSON Schema)

I designed a weight-based scoring system where each question's options dynamically affect scores across four dimensions.

Interactive Logic Implementation

Dynamic Weight Calculation

Real-time array score computation, ultimately mapping to the corresponding personality key (e.g., TSDM = Strategist Elephant).

Social Matching Algorithm

Logic to determine compatible (Dining Buddy) and incompatible (Dining Disaster) combinations. For example, the logic-driven "Logic Swan" and the intuition-driven "Party Pup" are algorithmically exclusive.

AI-Collaborative Workflow

Phase 1: Logic Architecture

(Human) Define problem & goals, establish the "four-dimension dining psychology" model inspired by MBTI.

(Output) 16-personality logic skeleton.

Phase 2: Parallel Production

Track A — Content: Prompt Chain design → AI generates 16 personality drafts → Human refinement → Final copy & JSON.

Track B — Visual: Set visual anchor → Prompt stress testing → Midjourney generation → Fine-tuning → 16 consistent character cards.

Phase 3: Full-stack Integration

(Human) Design scoring weight system & social matching algorithm.

(AI - Copilot) Pair programming for JavaScript logic & CSS.

(Output) Final product: RWD interactive web app.

Reflection

A Paradigm Shift in the Designer's Role

This project validated that in the AI era, the designer's battlefield has shifted from simply "producing screens" to "defining problems" and "architecting systems."

From Creator to Curator

AI can generate infinite copy and images, but only humans can decide what's "genuinely funny" and what's "stylistically consistent." Aesthetic judgment and insight became the most important filters.

The Power of Rapid Prototyping

What previously took weeks of planning and design was compressed to days through AI collaboration, freeing time to focus on interaction experience and creative detail refinement.

This project isn't just a quiz — it's a manifesto for the future of work. When we master the rhythm of dancing with AI, individual creativity knows no bounds.
