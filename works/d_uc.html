Professional Live Streaming Solution

2019 - 2021

Stakeholders: Product Manager, RD Team, Marketing.

This project aimed to develop a new series of live streaming products to expand market share and penetrate untapped segments. The core focus was on translating complex user needs into functional hardware-software solutions, optimizing the front panel ergonomics and intuitive software UI for professional broadcasters.

Key Achievements: The series became a commercial breakthrough, achieving the highest sales volume recorded for that year and receiving multiple international design awards for its innovation in user interaction.

The resulting product line offers a versatile solution for scenarios including remote conferences, live education, and high-end content creation, consisting of two flagship models and three OEM products.

Market Research

Decoding the Essence of Professional Live Streaming:
Trends and Components.

I performed extensive research into various webcasting modalities, categorizing them based on historical evolution and technical requirements. Our analysis identified two decisive factors driving the industry: Interaction Depth and Equipment Expertise.

The research unveiled a significant shift: nearly all segments are moving toward enhanced interactivity and broadcast-grade production quality, as illustrated by the "Program Live Streaming" category in our strategic matrix.

The Shifting Landscape: Unveiling Three Strategic Trends

By mapping industry developments onto a four-quadrant matrix, we visualized the following trajectories:

1. Traditional Media Transformation: Legacy channels are pivoting toward digital platforms to increase audience engagement.
2. Creator Economy Professionalization: Independent creators are upgrading to broadcast-level equipment to improve content quality.
3. Education & Seminars: The pandemic accelerated the demand for high-interactivity and rich content in remote learning environments.

Strategic Targeting: Bridging the Gap in Technical Barriers.

While live streaming appears simple on the surface, the backend technology is highly complex. Our technical analysis showed that while platform-level interaction is centralized, frontend hardware-software integration is our core competitive advantage.

We strategically focused on the segments (highlighted in yellow and blue) where users face significant challenges in "cross-border" operations—moving from simple setups to professional production levels.

The Barrier to Entry: Addressing the Struggles of "The Third Quadrant."

Most users begin with a smartphone or laptop. However, upgrading to the next level introduces an overwhelming surge in technical difficulty. Our goal was to eliminate these "friction points."

Problem Discovery & Solutions

Identified Pain Points: Equipment Complexity & Operational Cognitive Load.

Q1. High Technical Barriers in Hardware Installation

The proliferation of devices leads to complex cabling and adapter requirements. Non-professional users often struggle with IO specifications and equipment synchronization, leading to errors during live events.

Q2. Lack of Production Expertise & Specialized Staff

Professional broadcasting typically requires a team (directors, sound engineers, etc.). Individual creators lack the staff and knowledge to manage multi-camera switching and audio mixing effectively.

Q3. Complexity in Multi-Platform Distribution

Traditional "Master Control" operations for routing streams to various channels require deep networking knowledge, posing a significant barrier for average users.

Solution 1. Hardware-Software Consolidation

Reducing Setup Complexity through IO Integration

In collaboration with the PM team, we developed an all-in-one I/O interface that integrates video capture, audio mixing, and control surfaces into a single machine. Using a "Music Education" scenario as a case study: we transformed a fragmented setup into a streamlined, single-cable connection experience.

Solution 2. Intelligent Production Automation

Scene-Based Workflow for Solo Operators

1. Professional Directing Made Simple: We redesigned the preview and switching interface, allowing users to monitor and switch feeds intuitively via a tablet or laptop.

2. Automated Visual Assets: Introduced innovative "Scene-Based" operations. Users can trigger broadcast-quality lower-thirds, graphics, and multi-camera transitions with a single tap.

3. Smart Audio Mixing: Added an "Audio-Follow-Video" (AFV) function and intelligent presets for novice users, significantly reducing cognitive load.

Solution 3. Seamless Cloud Distribution

Unified Stream Management

We integrated common streaming protocols and platforms into a simplified UI. With support for OAuth login, RTMP stream keys, and custom API integration, users can go live on multiple platforms simultaneously without technical expertise.
